The It's Not Big Dough Story

Pizza made with a bit of heart in it.

We're a London-based Neapolitan pizza pop-up, making pies just like nonna used to — she's from Naples, and this whole thing started with her recipes and a wood-fired oven.

Around 1 in 4 people in the UK deal with mental health struggles, something we've felt ourselves. "It's Not Big Dough" is a reminder that it's not big to struggle on your own, either. Making pizza has always brought us joy through the harder moments — we hope a slice can do the same for you.

We run residencies and private events all year round, any size or shape welcomed. We're also working towards supporting mental health charities as we grow.
